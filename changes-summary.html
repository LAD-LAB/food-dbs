Corrections to releases 2026.08 and 2026.10
MODIFIED trnL g/h and c/d — eight mislabelled records omitted 2,116 → 2,109 (g/h), 4,520 → 4,515 (c/d)
  • How it was found: a downstream wastewater dataset re-assigned with v2026.10 lost its most abundant lettuce ASV (50 bp, over 160,000 reads) to class level. The g/h record PP081760.1, labelled Lonicera japonica (Caprifoliaceae), carries a lettuce-type sequence, so lettuce reads matched records from two families and the assignment collapsed. It arrived with the Aug 2026 build and is in the deposited 2026.08 and 2026.10 releases.
  • What was checked: the amplicon between the primer sites was compared across all g/h records. Seven groups of records from different families have identical sequence. Seven of the eight records below are in those groups; the eighth, PP081791.1, comes from the same submission. Each was compared with its own species, genus and family and BLASTed against NCBI. None was checked against a physical voucher.
  • New in the Aug 2026 release (3): PP081760.1 Lonicera japonica (lettuce-type); PP081769.1 Coix lacryma-jobi (identical to the ginger-family Zingiber mioga; 13+ edits from every other Coix); PP081791.1 Aloe vera (100% identical to Rorippa cantoniensis, mustard family, including another record from the same submission).
  • Present since at least the 2025 reference (5): GU134556.1 Elaeis guineensis (identical to 13 Asteraceae records); KX667944.1 Sambucus nigra (identical to Lathyrus sylvestris); MK797493.1 Peperomia pellucida (identical to dozens of Moraceae records); DQ221163.1 Sesamum indicum (identical to Apocynaceae records); NC_057307.1 Eugenia brasiliensis (next card). All five were also flagged by the September 2026 label audit, and are in trnL c/d as well.
  • Effect: g/h loses one species, Peperomia pellucida, which has no other usable g/h record (now in known-absent-species.csv). c/d loses Eugenia brasiliensis. 12SV5 is unchanged. The evidence for each omission is in data/inputs/omit-accessions.csv.
MODIFIED NC_057307.1 — the RefSeq Eugenia brasiliensis plastome is not a Eugenia genome replaced by MN095407.1 in g/h
  • The record: a RefSeq complete chloroplast genome (160,802 bp), marked PROVISIONAL and identical to GenBank MT900596.1 (Yunnan Institute of Tropical Crops, submitted Aug 2020). Neither record carries a voucher, isolate or publication.
  • Evidence it is Malpighiaceae: its rbcL is 99–100% identical to Malpighiaceae (Aspidopterys, Malpighia, Hiptage) in BLAST with no Myrtaceae in the top 30 hits; its matK is 99% identical to Aspidopterys concava. The genus within Malpighiaceae was not determined.
  • Evidence from other E. brasiliensis records: the independent plastome MN095407.1 (Rodrigues et al. 2020) is only 94% identical to it in rbcL and 84% in matK, while 11 other E. brasiliensis records from at least four submissions (vouchers ICN 167416 and Lucas 126, isolate VCS35082) match MN095407.1 at 99.2–100%.
  • The fix: MN095407.1 is the E. brasiliensis g/h record (98 bp, forward-primer-first, identical to E. stipitata and E. uniflora in that region). c/d is built by alignment, not primer matching, so E. brasiliensis leaves c/d until a rebuild. MN095407.1's own GenBank record has no voucher either; its support is the paper and the agreement above.

Quality control
MODIFIED qc_reference_build.R — check 11: cross-family identical amplicons
  • The check: the gate now fails when a record that is new since the previous build has the same primer-trimmed amplicon as a record from a different family, and warns about groups that already existed. Applies to the markers with primer sites (g/h and 12SV5). Such a record makes every read of that sequence unassignable below class, and one of the two is almost always mislabelled.
  • Tested against the bad data: run on the v2026.10 g/h files against May 2026 it fails on PP081760.1 and PP081769.1 (and on the correct Cichorium endivia record that sits in the same group as the mislabelled Elaeis). On v2026.11 it passes: no two families share an amplicon.
  • Still open: on 12SV5 it warns about six pre-existing groups. Two contain records the September audit already flagged (AM931025.1 Epinephelus marginatus with five Sciaenidae; NC_022490.1 Nesiarchus nasutus with four flatfish). They have not been reviewed and are not changed in this release.
  • Result for v2026.11: 0 FAIL, 4 WARN (Peperomia pellucida and Eugenia brasiliensis dropped from g/h and c/d respectively, the known Correlophus ciliatus control, and the 12SV5 groups).
Workflows
NEW data/inputs/omit-accessions.csv 11 rows
  • A single list of known-mislabelled accessions with, for each, the markers it applies to, what the sequence actually is, the evidence and the date. It replaces the hard-coded c/d vector (trnLCD_omit) in step CD7.
  • Read by: the pipeline (steps A7, CD7 and B5), Extend reference.Rmd (so a plastome or marker query cannot bring a listed record back; a plastome query for E. brasiliensis returns NC_057307.1) and the documentation. Includes MT900596.1, the GenBank twin of NC_057307.1.
Release naming & archive
MODIFIED Files renamed _v2026.11; the 2026.10 release is archived
  • All databases are named _v2026.11 (dada2- and qiime2-compatible; trnL, 12Sv5, trnLCD) to match the Zenodo version. The released 2026.10 files are in archive/oct2026/ under _Oct2026 names, which is where the QC gate looks for the previous build (qc_reference_build.R . _v2026.11 _Oct2026).
  • The 12SV5 files are unchanged in content and renamed so the release is a consistent set. The QIIME 2 files were regenerated from the DADA2 files after first confirming the generator reproduces the released 2026.10 QIIME 2 pairs byte for byte. All files are LF.
  • A zenodo-upload-v2026.11/ folder and zip were built with a README that opens with a 'Corrections to 2026.10 and 2026.08' section, plus SHA-256 checksums. The release was merged as e040875 and deposited on Zenodo as version 2026.11 (10.5281/zenodo.24144519); the README citation points to it.
