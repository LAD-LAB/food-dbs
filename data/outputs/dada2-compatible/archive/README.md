This folder is for archiving old versions of the trnL and 12Sv5 references. Files are named with the month and year they were created/uploaded to GitHub.

**Release versions** (named after the Zenodo version they match):

- `aug2026/` - the Aug 2026 release (Zenodo v2026.08), identical in content to the deposited files, stored with LF line endings (the deposited 12SV5 and trnL g/h files are CRLF; the Zenodo record holds the exact bytes). Superseded by `_v2026.10`, which is what sits in the marker folders.
- `may2026/`, `dateless/`, and the 2022 folders - earlier builds.

**Snapshots** (`aug2026-pre-*`): the reference as it stood immediately before each recovery step in Oct 2026 (`pre-recovery`, `pre-legacy-191`, `pre-cod-pollock-tuna`, `pre-sander`, `pre-gapcheck58`, `pre-names-plastome`). Working copies kept for provenance; they are not releases.
